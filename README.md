### Concept 2: Outcome batao, raasta nahi
* Outcome do, steps nahi:** “3 best free courses ki table banao” → AI khud research ka raasta choose kare.
* Agar aap pehle se **2 websites fix** kar dein aur woh galat hon, to result neat hoga lekin recommendation weak.
* **Check outcome se karo:** dekho final result ne required goal poora kiya ya nahi, na ke AI ne aapke steps follow kiye.
* **Steps tab do jab zaroori hon:** jaise “sirf official sites use karo.” Ghalat tab hai jab sirf tension ki wajah se har step pehle se define kar dein.
/////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////Delegation Brief (concise, bina table ke)
Delegation Brief kaam shuru hone se *pehle* likha jane wala chhe sawalon ka work order hai, taake kaam ke baad aap ke paas check karne ke liye kuch ho.
Chhe sawal yeh hain:
1. **Outcome:** kaam khatam hone par kya mojood ho.
2. **Context:** aap ke baare mein woh facts jo AI nahi jaanta, jaise "main beginner hoon".
3. **Constraints:** kaam par hadden aur shartein, jaise "sirf free courses".
4. **Authority:** AI kya khud tay kare aur kis par ruk kar poochay.
5. **Deliverable:** nateeja kis shakal mein chahiye, jaise table ya document.
6. **Verification:** qabool karne se pehle kya check hoga, jaise "jo confirm na ho use unverified likho".
Yaad rakhne ki baatein:
 Log aksar **Authority** aur **Verification** chhor dete hain. Authority na ho to AI chupke se faislay kar leta hai, aur Verification na ho to nateeja bina jaanche qabool ho jata hai.
- Verification kaam se *pehle* likhi jati hai.
- Yeh form nahi, sochne ka tool hai. Nateeja nirash kare to chhe mein se koi ek sawal khali tha.
- Context facts hain, Constraints shartein hain.
**Exam tip:** scenario mein AI ne chupke se faisla kiya ya nateeja bina jaanche chala gaya, to jawab aksar missing Authority ya Verification hota hai.
"Aage" likh dein to Concept 4 shuru karta hoon.
/////////////////////////////////////////////////////////Concept 4: Research (short)
AI yaad se bolta hai (purana) ya search karta hai (taaza). Price, date, link ke liye search chahiye, isliye brief mein "current information use karo" likho.
Search se aayi baat ke saath source hota hai jo khul sake. Bina source ke current claim ka matlab search nahi hua.
AI ka "maine search kiya" kehna sirf ishara hai. Asal saboot source aur uski tareekh hai.
Source taaza ho yeh zaroori nahi, tareekh khud dekho.
Citation verification nahi. Page khol kar woh sentence dhoondna verification hai.
Deep research tab use karo jab final cheez khud ek report ho Khud ak report yani ak bara tafseli jawab jo mukhtlf chezon pr mushtamil ho. Chhote fact ke liye aam search kaafi hai.
Exam tip: AI ne bina source ke taaza fact diya, to source maango aur page khol kar check karo.
